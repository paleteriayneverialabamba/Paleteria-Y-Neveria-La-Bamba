Our Story

Made fresh, served with a smile.

La Bamba brings the flavors of a Mexican paletería to Atlantic Avenue — hand-dipped paletas, shaved-ice raspados in a dozen flavors, and the fresh street corn we're known for.

Everything is built to order, from a classic mango raspado to a fully loaded churro sundae, right in the heart of Ducktown.
